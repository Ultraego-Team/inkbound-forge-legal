Tink Tink Boom: Dig Survivor - Delete your data

App: Tink Tink Boom: Dig Survivor, on iPhone and Android
Developer: UltraEgo
Contact: support@tinktinkboom.com

The game has no login and no account with a name, email address or password. Your game data is linked to a random Player ID that the game creates on your device. This page explains how to delete that data in the game, and how to ask us to delete the data our partners hold.

1. Delete the data on your device (in the game)

  1. Open the game and tap Settings.
  2. Tap DELETE LOCAL DATA (you can also use DELETE MY DATA on the privacy screen).
  3. Confirm when the game asks.
  4. Restart the game. It begins fresh.

This erases everything the game stored on your device: your save and backups, the Player ID (kept in the iPhone Keychain on iPhone, or in the game's own app storage on Android), settings, privacy choices, statistics, crash reports and ad limits. It does not delete data that was already sent to our partners. For that, see section 2.

Before you delete, copy your Player ID if you want to ask for partner data to be deleted (Settings, Copy). After deleting, the old Player ID is gone from your device.

2. Ask us to delete data held by our partners

Email support@tinktinkboom.com with the subject "Tink Tink Boom data deletion" and include your Player ID (Settings in the game, tap Copy). If you can, also give the app version and the dates you played.

  • We delete your RevenueCat customer record (the purchase record linked to your Player ID) within 30 days.
  • We pass your request to Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Meta, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks listed in the Privacy Policy.
  • Each partner also offers its own access and deletion options under its own privacy policy, linked in the Privacy Policy.

3. What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records: Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) keep records of your in-app purchases under their own policies. After a deletion request, RevenueCat keeps only the purchase records that tax and accounting law requires, for as long as that law requires.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: event data is kept for no more than 14 months, then deleted.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are deleted after 90 days.
  • Meta, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks: each keeps the data it received under its own privacy policy until it deletes it, or until it acts on the deletion request we pass on.

4. More information

The Privacy Policy lists every partner, what each one receives and why. See also the support page and the Terms of Use.
