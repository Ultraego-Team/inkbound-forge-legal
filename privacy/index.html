Inkbound Forge - Privacy Policy

Publisher: UltraEgo

Contact: ultraego.dev@gmail.com

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy describes the Android version of Inkbound Forge ("the game") as it is built today. It will be updated before any change described in section 9 is released.

1. Summary

  • The game does not send any data from your device to the publisher or to any third party.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics upload and no crash report upload.
  • Game data stays in files on your device. You can erase it at any time with the in-game DELETE LOCAL DATA control.

2. Information the game stores on your device

The game stores the following only in local files in the game's private storage on your device:

  • Game progress, settings and your choices on the first-launch choices screen.
  • Local diagnostic files, such as performance and crash records written to a folder on your device. These files are not uploaded. The game may read your device model and graphics hardware name to choose a suitable graphics quality level; this is used only on the device.
  • An age band (see section 4).

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or share any personal information. The game does not create accounts. It does not use your advertising ID, location, camera, microphone, contacts or files outside its own storage.

4. Age gate

On first launch the game asks for a birth month and year to work out an age band. The birth date is not stored. Only the resulting age band is kept on your device. For players below the minimum age that applies in their region, optional analytics, crash and ads settings are switched off. In the current build these settings only affect local files and nothing is sent.

5. Notifications

The game can show local notifications, such as reminders. They are scheduled on your device and do not use a push server. You can turn them off in your Android system settings.

6. Google Play

The game is delivered through Google Play. Google handles installation and updates, and will handle any purchase if purchases are added in future. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policy, which are separate from this policy. The game's Android permissions include network access, vibration, notifications and Google Play Billing; the game does not currently use the network to send your data anywhere.

7. Deleting your data

Open Settings in the game and choose DELETE LOCAL DATA to erase the game's local files, including progress and local diagnostic files. Uninstalling the game also removes its local files from your device.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children below the minimum age in your region. See section 4 for how the age gate works.

9. Changes

This policy describes a build with no online service, no ads and no real-money purchases. We will update this policy, and Google Play data disclosures, before any online service, ads, analytics upload or real purchases are added.
