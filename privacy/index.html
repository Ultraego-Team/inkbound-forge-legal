Inkbound Forge - Privacy Policy

Publisher: UltraEgo

Contact: ultraego.dev@gmail.com

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy describes Inkbound Forge ("the game") on Android and iPhone as it is built today. It will be updated before any change described in section 9 is released.

1. Summary

  • The game does not send any data from your device to the publisher. When you buy or restore an in-app purchase, purchase data goes to Apple or Google and to RevenueCat, our purchase processor (section 6). Nothing else leaves your device.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics upload and no crash report upload.
  • Game data stays in files on your device. You can erase it at any time with the in-game DELETE LOCAL DATA control.

2. Information the game stores on your device

The game stores the following only in local files in the game's private storage on your device:

  • Game progress, settings and your choices on the first-launch choices screen.
  • Local diagnostic files, such as performance and crash records written to a folder on your device. These files are not uploaded. The game may read your device model and graphics hardware name to choose a suitable graphics quality level; this is used only on the device.
  • An age band (see section 4).

3. Information we collect

We do not collect or receive any personal information ourselves. The only data shared with a third party is the purchase data described in section 6. The game does not create accounts. It does not use your advertising ID, location, camera, microphone, contacts or files outside its own storage.

4. Age gate

On first launch the game asks for a birth month and year to work out an age band. The birth date is not stored. Only the resulting age band is kept on your device. For players below the minimum age that applies in their region, optional analytics, crash and ads settings are switched off. In the current build these settings only affect local files and nothing is sent.

5. Notifications

The game can show local notifications, such as reminders. They are scheduled on your device and do not use a push server. You can turn them off in your device settings.

6. App stores and in-app purchases

The game is delivered through the Apple App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. Apple and Google handle installation, updates and payment for any in-app purchase. We never see or receive your payment card or bank details. Apple and Google process information under their own terms and privacy policies, which are separate from this policy.

Where in-app purchases are available in your version of the game, we use RevenueCat, Inc. as our service provider (processor) to check and deliver them. When you buy, or tap Restore Purchases, the game sends RevenueCat:

  • a random ID the game creates for your game data, which is not linked to your name, email or store account;
  • the purchase record from Apple or Google: the product, transaction ID, price, currency, purchase date and store country;
  • basic technical information needed to make the connection: app version, operating system version, device model, language and IP address.

RevenueCat confirms each purchase with Apple or Google and tells the game which purchases are valid, refunded or can be restored, so the game delivers each purchase once, removes items from a refunded purchase and restores items you own on a new device. The game does not give RevenueCat your advertising ID or other device identifiers. RevenueCat processes this data only on our behalf, under its own privacy policy (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and under its agreement with us. The game's Android permissions include network access, vibration, notifications and Google Play Billing; the network is used only for the purchase checks described here.

7. Deleting your data

Open Settings in the game and choose DELETE LOCAL DATA to erase the game's local files, including progress and local diagnostic files. Uninstalling the game also removes its local files from your device. Deleting local data does not delete purchase records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat. To ask us to delete the purchase records RevenueCat holds for your game, email us; Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children below the minimum age in your region. See section 4 for how the age gate works.

9. Changes

This policy describes a build with no online service of our own, no ads and no analytics upload; the only data that leaves your device is the purchase data in section 6. We will update this policy, and the App Store and Google Play data disclosures, before any online service, ads or analytics upload are added.
