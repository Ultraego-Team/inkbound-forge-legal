Tink Tink Boom: Dig Survivor - Privacy Policy

Publisher and data controller: UltraEgo

Contact: support@tinktinkboom.com

Last updated: 21 November 2026. This version applies to Tink Tink Boom 1.0 on iPhone and on Android.

This policy explains what the game Tink Tink Boom ("the game") does with information about you, which outside services receive it, and how you can control or delete it. The game has no login and creates no account with a name, email address or password.

1. Summary

  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • The game sends information to these outside services: Apple (App Store and in-app purchases on iPhone), Google Play (Play Store and in-app purchases on Android), RevenueCat (checks purchases), Unity LevelPlay by ironSource (the ad mediator that picks which ad network shows each optional rewarded ad), the ad networks listed in section 4, Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and the Meta SDK (measuring our ads on Facebook and Instagram).
  • The data types sent are device identifiers, a user ID, coarse location, purchase history, product interaction, advertising data, usage data and diagnostics (crash, performance and other diagnostic data). Section 3 lists each one and what it is used for.
  • On iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in the iPhone tracking prompt. On Android there is no tracking prompt: the Android advertising ID can be read unless you have deleted or reset it in your Android settings, and the game still applies your privacy choices and your answer on Google's consent form.
  • In every region, the ad mediator, the ad networks and the Meta SDK start only after the game has your privacy choice. On iPhone, the Meta SDK also waits for your answer to the tracking prompt.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports follow the same privacy choices: in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and South Korea they stay off until you say yes. Everywhere else they are on from the first launch and you can turn them off at any time in Settings, Privacy Choices.
  • We do not sell your information for money. We never receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details from the game.
  • Deleting local data in the game removes the save on your device. Purchase records stay with Apple or Google Play and RevenueCat, and our partners keep the data they received under their own policies. Section 12 and the Delete your data page explain how to ask for deletion.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Game progress, settings and the choices you make on the privacy screens.
  • A random Player ID created by the game. It is not your name, email or store account. You can see and copy the first characters of it in Settings. On iPhone it is kept in the iPhone Keychain; on Android it is kept in the game's own app storage.
  • On iPhone, an age band from Apple's age range answer, if Apple provides one (see section 11). The game does not ask for or store your birth date on any device.
  • Local diagnostic and performance files. The game may read your device model and graphics hardware to pick a suitable graphics quality.

6. Advertising identifier and tracking

On iPhone: before the iPhone tracking prompt the game shows a short explanation screen. If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA) can be read by the ad networks, the Meta SDK and RevenueCat. If you do not allow tracking, no one receives the advertising identifier, ads are not personalised with it, and Meta uses Apple's privacy-preserving measurement instead. The ad SDKs and the Meta SDK still read the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and other device information, for example to limit how often an ad is shown, measure ads and prevent fraud. You can change your answer at any time under iPhone Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

On Android: there is no tracking prompt. The Android advertising ID (AAID) can be read by the ad networks, the Meta SDK and RevenueCat unless you have deleted or reset it in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads, or Settings, Google, Ads, depending on the phone). The game still applies its own privacy choices and your answer on Google's consent form: if you decline personalised ads, ads are not personalised. The ad SDKs and the Meta SDK also read the app set ID and other device information, for example to limit how often an ad is shown, measure ads and prevent fraud.

7. Ads

The game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch. There are no banner, full-screen or app-open ads. Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), operated by Unity Technologies, acts as the ad mediator: for each ad it asks the networks in section 4 and shows the ad that wins. A reward is given only when the network confirms the ad was watched. In every region, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks start only after the game has your privacy choice: your answer on Google's consent form (UMP) where the form is shown, and your privacy settings in the game. The game passes your choice to LevelPlay and the networks. If you decline personalised ads, you can still watch ads, but they are not personalised. You can change your ad choices at any time: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland with AD PRIVACY OPTIONS in Settings in the game; in US states with the Do not sell or share my data choice in the game's privacy settings; and on iPhone under iPhone Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, or on Android by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads, or Settings, Google, Ads). Players below the minimum age in their region get only non-personalised ads. The ad networks act as independent controllers for the data they receive and use it under their own privacy policies, linked in section 4.

8. Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Apple on iPhone and through Google Play (Google Play Billing) on Android. The store handles payment, and we never see your payment card or bank details. RevenueCat, Inc. processes purchase data on our behalf, under its agreement with us and its privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy). Apple and Google process information under their own terms and privacy policies (Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy; Google: policies.google.com/privacy).

9. Usage statistics and crash reports

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are provided by Google LLC. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and South Korea the game keeps both off until you allow them (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland through Google's consent form) and sends nothing to them before that. Everywhere else both are on from the first launch, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it, and you can turn either off at any time in Settings, Privacy Choices. The game tells Firebase not to use the data for advertising: advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always set to denied. If you turn usage statistics off later, collection stops and the analytics ID on your device is reset. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy; Firebase privacy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

9a. Measuring our ads (Meta)

The Meta SDK is provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). In every region the game starts it only after it has your privacy choice, and on iPhone your answer to the tracking prompt. It also follows your usage statistics choice: in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and South Korea it does not start until you allow usage statistics, and everywhere it stops when you turn usage statistics off in Settings, Privacy Choices. It helps us see which of our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to installs and purchases. If you turn usage statistics off later, the game stops sending events to Meta. Meta's privacy policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy.

10. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Purchases: to perform our contract with you (delivering what you bought) and to meet legal duties such as tax records.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports, Meta ad measurement and personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.
  • Non-personalised rewarded ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game, and your consent where the law requires it for storing or reading information on your device (asked through Google's consent form).

11. Children and age

The game does not ask for your age. On iPhone, where Apple's Declared Age Range service gives the game an age range for your Apple account, the game keeps only an age band on your device. For players below the minimum age that applies in their region, usage statistics, crash reports and personalised ads are switched off and the tracking prompt is never shown. On Android the game does not ask for your age either. The game is not directed at children below the minimum age in their region.

12. How long information is kept, and deleting it

  • On your device: until you delete it. In Settings, DELETE LOCAL DATA (or DELETE MY DATA on the privacy screen) asks once for confirmation, then erases everything the game stored on this device: your save and backups, the Player ID (kept in the iPhone Keychain on iPhone, or in the game's own app storage on Android), settings, privacy choices, statistics, crash reports and ad limits. A restart begins fresh. Uninstalling the game removes its files from your device. On Android this includes the Player ID, unless the phone restores the game from an Android backup. Deleting local data does not delete data that was already sent to the services in section 4.
  • Purchases: Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) and RevenueCat keep your purchase records. Apple and Google keep them under their own policies. RevenueCat keeps them while they are needed to restore your purchases and handle refunds, and as long as tax and accounting law requires. To have your RevenueCat customer record deleted, email us with your Player ID (Settings, Copy) and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: event data is kept for no more than 14 months. Turning usage statistics off in Settings stops collection and resets the analytics ID. To ask for deletion of data already sent, email us with your Player ID, the app version and the dates you played.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are deleted by Crashlytics after 90 days.
  • Meta: Meta keeps app event data under its own policy. Turning usage statistics off stops new events. You can manage off-Facebook activity in your Facebook or Instagram account settings, or email us and we will pass a deletion request to Meta.
  • Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks: each keeps the data it received under its own privacy policy (linked in section 4) and offers its own access and deletion options. On iPhone, turning off tracking stops them receiving the advertising identifier; on Android, deleting your advertising ID in your Android settings does the same. You can also email us and we will pass your request to LevelPlay and the networks.

For any deletion request, email support@tinktinkboom.com and include your Player ID if you can. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your data page.

13. Sharing, sale and international transfers

We share information only with the services in section 4. We do not sell your information for money. The information Unity LevelPlay, the ad networks and Meta receive may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, or as a "sale", under some US state laws. To opt out, turn on Do not sell or share my data in the game's privacy settings, and on iPhone do not allow tracking in the tracking prompt (you can change it later under Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking), or on Android delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. These services may process information in the United States and other countries. Where EEA, UK or Swiss law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of sharing for targeted advertising. Email us to use these rights; we answer within 30 days and do not treat you differently for using them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

15. Notifications

The game can show local notifications, such as reminders. They are scheduled on your device and do not use a push server. You can turn them off in your device settings.

16. Changes

We update this policy, and the App Store privacy answers and the Google Play Data safety section, before the game starts using a new service or a new kind of information. The date at the top shows the latest version.
