Inkbound Forge - Privacy Policy

Publisher and data controller: UltraEgo

Contact: ultraego.dev@gmail.com

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This version applies from the release of Inkbound Forge 1.0 on iPhone.

This policy explains what the game Inkbound Forge ("the game") does with information about you, which service providers it uses, and how you can control or delete that information. The game has no login and creates no account with a name, email address or password.

1. Summary

  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • The game uses five outside services: Apple (App Store and in-app purchases), RevenueCat (checks purchases), Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads), Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports).
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are only sent if you allow them. Where the law requires your agreement first (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and South Korea), they stay off until you say yes.
  • Ads are personalised only if you allow it, including in the iPhone tracking prompt. Players below the minimum age in their region never get personalised ads, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • We do not sell your information for money. We never receive your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details from the game.
  • You can change your choices in the game's Settings at any time and delete your data as described in section 10.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Game progress, settings and the choices you make on the privacy screens.
  • A random Player ID created by the game. It is not your name, email or store account. You can see and copy the first characters of it in Settings.
  • An age band (see section 9). Your birth date is not stored.
  • Local diagnostic and performance files. The game may read your device model and graphics hardware to pick a suitable graphics quality.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment card or bank details. RevenueCat, Inc. processes purchase data only on our behalf, under its agreement with us and its privacy policy (revenuecat.com/privacy). Apple processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Ads

The game shows only rewarded ads that you choose to watch. They are served by Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before the first ad; if you decline, you can still watch ads, but they are not personalised. On iPhone, the game asks your permission before the system tracking prompt; if you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not used and ads are not personalised. You can change your ad choices at any time: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland with AD PRIVACY OPTIONS in Settings in the game; in US states with the Do not sell or share my data choice in the game's privacy settings; and everywhere on iPhone under Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Players below the minimum age get only non-personalised ads marked as directed at children. How Google uses data from ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. Usage statistics and crash reports

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are provided by Google LLC. The game turns both off until you allow them, and sends nothing to them before that. The game tells Firebase not to use the data for advertising: advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are always set to denied. If you turn usage statistics off later, collection stops and the analytics ID on your device is reset. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy; Firebase privacy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Purchases: to perform our contract with you (delivering what you bought) and to meet legal duties such as tax records.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports and personalised ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.
  • Non-personalised rewarded ads and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in funding a free game, and your consent where the law requires it for storing information on your device (asked through Google's consent form).

8. Sharing, sale and international transfers

We share information only with the services in section 3. We do not sell your information for money. If you allow personalised ads, the information Google AdMob receives may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under some US state laws. To opt out, turn on Do not sell or share my data in the game's privacy settings, or do not allow tracking in the iPhone prompt (you can change it later under Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking). Google and RevenueCat may process information in the United States and other countries. Where EEA, UK or Swiss law requires it, these transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

9. Children and the age gate

On first launch the game asks for a birth month and year to work out an age band. The birth date is not stored; only the age band is kept on your device. For players below the minimum age that applies in their region, usage statistics, crash reports and personalised ads are switched off and the tracking prompt is never shown. The game is not directed at children below the minimum age in their region.

10. How long information is kept, and deleting it

  • On your device: until you delete it. In Settings, DELETE LOCAL DATA erases your progress and local files; the privacy screen's delete option erases your privacy choices, statistics queue, crash files and ad limits. Uninstalling the game removes its files from your device.
  • RevenueCat: kept while it is needed to restore your purchases and handle refunds, and as long as tax and accounting law requires. To have it deleted, email us with your Player ID (Settings, Copy) and we will delete your RevenueCat customer record within 30 days. Apple keeps its own purchase records under its own policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: event data is kept for no more than 14 months. Turning usage statistics off in Settings stops collection and resets the analytics ID. To ask for deletion of data already sent, email us; because the game does not send your Player ID to Google, we may need the app version and the date range you played to find it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are deleted by Crashlytics after 90 days. They are not linked to your Player ID.
  • Google AdMob: Google acts as an independent controller for personalised ads. You can reset or block the advertising identifier in the iPhone settings, and use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy or Google's privacy help to ask Google for access or deletion. You can also email us and we will pass your request to Google.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of sharing for targeted advertising. Email us to use these rights; we answer within 30 days and do not treat you differently for using them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

12. Notifications

The game can show local notifications, such as reminders. They are scheduled on your device and do not use a push server. You can turn them off in your device settings.

13. Android

This policy also covers the Android version from Google Play. Where a service in section 3 is not yet switched on in the Android version, no data goes to it from Android.

14. Changes

We update this policy, and the App Store privacy answers, before the game starts using a new service or a new kind of information. The date at the top shows the latest version.
