Inkbound Forge - Support

Need help with the game? Email ultraego.dev@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 3 working days.

What to include

Your device model, Android or iOS version, the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the game) and what happened, with a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device, inside the game's own data. There is no cloud save yet.

Does a phone backup keep my progress? Yes on iPhone. An iCloud or computer backup includes your progress and Player ID, so restoring that backup, or moving to a new iPhone from it, brings them back.

What does deleting and reinstalling the game keep? On the same iPhone, your Player ID is kept in the iPhone Keychain, but your progress, gems and items are removed with the game unless you restore the iPhone from a backup. After a reinstall, tap Restore purchases (Settings, Account, or the bottom of the Shop). It brings back the Ad-Free Card, Daily Gem Card, Chapter Fund and Chapter Fund Plus, and a Battle Pass Premium or Gilded Ledger from this season and a Monthly Card with days left while the store lists them for your Player ID. Gems and packs are not restored, and nothing is given twice. On Android, uninstalling the game removes your progress.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in the game and choose DELETE LOCAL DATA. On iPhone this also removes the Player ID kept in the Keychain.

A purchase did not arrive. Restart the game first. If it is still missing, email us with the order number from Google Play or Apple. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
