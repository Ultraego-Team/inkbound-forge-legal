Tink Tink Boom: Dig Survivor - Support

Need help with the game? Email support@tinktinkboom.com. We aim to answer within 3 working days.

What to include

Your Player ID (Settings in the game, tap Copy), your device model, iOS or Android version, the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what happened, with a screenshot if you can. The Support screen in Settings copies the Player ID, build and platform in one tap.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device, inside the game's own data. Nothing is kept in the cloud in 1.0.

Does a phone backup keep my progress? Yes on iPhone. An iCloud or computer backup includes your progress and Player ID, so restoring that backup, or moving to a new iPhone from it, brings them back.

What does deleting and reinstalling the game keep? On the same iPhone, your Player ID is kept in the iPhone Keychain, but your progress, gems and items are removed with the game unless you restore the iPhone from a backup. After a reinstall, tap Restore purchases (Settings, Account, or the bottom of the Shop). It brings back the Ad-Free Card, Daily Gem Card, Chapter Fund and Chapter Fund Plus, and a Battle Pass Premium or Gilded Ledger from this season and a Monthly Card with days left while the store lists them for your Player ID. Gems and packs are not restored, and nothing is given twice. On Android, uninstalling the game removes your progress.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in the game and choose DELETE LOCAL DATA (or DELETE MY DATA on the privacy screen). The game asks once for confirmation, then erases everything it stored on this device: your save and backups, the Player ID kept in the iPhone Keychain, settings, privacy choices, statistics, crash reports and ad limits. Nothing is kept in the cloud in 1.0. Restart the game and it begins fresh.
