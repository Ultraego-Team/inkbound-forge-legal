Inkbound Forge - Support

Need help with the game? Email ultraego.dev@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 3 working days.

What to include

Your Player ID (Settings in the game, tap Copy), your device model, iOS or Android version, the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what happened, with a screenshot if you can. The Support screen in Settings copies the Player ID, build and platform in one tap.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Uninstalling the game removes it.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in the game and choose DELETE LOCAL DATA to erase your progress and local files; the privacy screen's delete option also clears your privacy choices, statistics, crash files and ad limits on the device.

How do I delete data held by your service providers? Email us with the subject "Delete my data" and your Player ID. Within 30 days we delete your purchase record at RevenueCat and ask Google to delete analytics data from Google Analytics for Firebase; crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics are not linked to you and are deleted after 90 days. For Google AdMob, reset or block the advertising identifier in iPhone Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking, and use Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy; we pass on any request you send us. Apple keeps its own purchase records. Details are in the Privacy Policy, section 10.

How do I change my privacy or ad choices? Settings in the game: the privacy choices screen for usage statistics and crash reports, AD PRIVACY OPTIONS in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and Do not sell or share my data in US states. On iPhone, tracking is under Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

A purchase did not arrive. Restart the game, then tap Restore Purchases in the shop. If it is still missing, email us with your Player ID and the order number from Apple or Google Play. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
