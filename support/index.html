Inkbound Forge - Support

Need help with the game? Email ultraego.dev@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 3 working days.

What to include

Your device model, Android or iOS version, the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the game) and what happened, with a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Uninstalling the game removes it.

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in the game and choose DELETE LOCAL DATA.

A purchase did not arrive. Restart the game first. If it is still missing, email us with the order number from Google Play or Apple. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.
